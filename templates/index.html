BUILD YOUR DEFENSE

Know the warning signs

Small habits make a big difference
⌁

Phishing links

Attackers imitate trusted brands to pressure you into sharing credentials or payment details.

  • Check the exact domain spelling.
  • Be wary of urgency and unexpected prizes.
▣

Safer browsing

HTTPS encrypts a connection, but it does not prove a website is honest or safe.

  • Open important sites from a bookmark.
  • Pause before opening unexpected links.
✳

Strong passwords

A long, unique password helps limit damage if another service has a breach.

  • Use a reputable password manager.
  • Turn on multi-factor authentication.
